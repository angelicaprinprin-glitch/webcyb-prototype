# WebCyb – Phishing Email Checker

A free website that tells you if an email you received is phishing.
Paste the email and WebCyb scans it for warning signs: suspicious links, urgent or threatening
language, requests for personal information, fake senders and risky attachments.

Everything runs in your browser. The emails you paste are never sent anywhere or stored.

Files: `index.html` (page), `style.css` (design), `detector.js` (phishing scanner), `app.js` (shows the result).
