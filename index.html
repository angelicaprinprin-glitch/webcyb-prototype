What is phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals send messages that pretend to come from someone you trust — your bank, a delivery company, your school, or a popular website. The goal is to trick you into clicking a dangerous link, opening a harmful attachment, or giving away personal information such as passwords, credit card numbers, or your identity.

  • Urgency or threats — “Your account will be closed in 24 hours!”
  • Requests for personal info — asking for passwords, bank or card details.
  • Suspicious links — web addresses that look almost right, but aren’t.
  • Fake senders — addresses like support@paypa1-secure.com.
